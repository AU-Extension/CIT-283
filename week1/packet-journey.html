Packet journey simulator

Follow a ping one step at a time. Before each step you predict what the network will do, then you watch it happen. You will see ARP, switch MAC tables and a router at work, exactly like Simulation mode in Packet Tracer.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose a ping. Start with Ping 1 (same network), then do Ping 2 (different network).
  2. Read the situation and pick your prediction. Only your first try counts toward the score.
  3. Watch the animation, then read the frame card and the tables on the right. They show what each device learned.
  4. Press Next step to continue. Your total score appears after both pings are complete.
  5. Use Restart this ping any time to try again with empty tables.
