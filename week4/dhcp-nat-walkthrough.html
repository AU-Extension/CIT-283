DHCP and NAT walkthrough

Two services make a small office network practical. DHCP hands every new device an address automatically, and NAT with PAT lets the whole office share one public IP address. Step through each one packet by packet and watch the router's tables fill in.

How to use this interactive
  1. Start on the DHCP tab. Press "Connect a new device", then use Next step to walk through Discover, Offer, Request and Acknowledge.
  2. Read the packet card under the diagram: it shows the source and destination addresses at each step.
  3. Change the excluded range and connect more devices to see which addresses the router leases.
  4. Switch to the NAT and PAT tab. Press Browse on each PC and watch the router rewrite the source address and port.
  5. Answer the quick check at the bottom when you finish both tabs.
