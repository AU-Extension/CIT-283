Small office network designer

Pick a Miami small-business client, read what the owner needs, and design the network: equipment, VLANs, wireless, subnet sizes, addressing and NAT. The designer checks your choices against the requirements, explains every point gained or lost, and produces a summary you can print.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose one of the clients below and read the requirements carefully. Counts matter.
  2. Work through the design form from top to bottom. The help line under each choice explains the term.
  3. Press "Check my design" to see your score out of 100 and specific feedback.
  4. Change your choices and check again as often as you like, then print the summary.
  5. Try all of the clients. Each one has a different trap.
