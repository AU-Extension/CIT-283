OSPF path visualizer

Sunrise Logistics now has five sites running OSPF in area 0. Change link speeds, set costs, or cut a link, then watch a router run the shortest path first (SPF) calculation step by step and build its routing table. A second tab shows how two routers become OSPF neighbors.

How to use this interactive
  1. Click a router to make it the source. That router will calculate its best path to every other site.
  2. Click a link (or its cost box) to change its bandwidth, give it a manual cost, or take it down.
  3. Press Next step to watch SPF confirm one router at a time, or Run all to see the result. Green links form the shortest path tree.
  4. Read the routing table at the bottom of the panel. Every O route shows [110/cost]: 110 is the OSPF administrative distance, and cost is the total of the outgoing interface costs.
  5. Open the Neighbor adjacency tab to bring up a link between two routers and see the OSPF neighbor states, including what happens when settings do not match.

Topology and link costs

Cost formula: reference bandwidth divided by interface bandwidth, never lower than 1. The default reference is 100 Mbps, so 100 Mbps and 1 Gbps links both cost 1.
